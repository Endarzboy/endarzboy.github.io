title: REFORM-3D accepted at NeurIPS 2026
date: 2026-11-09

Our paper **REFORM-3D**, a representation-centric evaluation framework for 3D medical vision foundation models, is accepted at **NeurIPS 2026** (Evaluations & Datasets Track). Read the [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10062) or visit the [project page](https://endarzboy.github.io/reform3d-website/).
