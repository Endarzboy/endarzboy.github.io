I am a **Postdoctoral Fellow at [Khalifa University](https://www.ku.ac.ae/)** in Abu Dhabi, UAE, where I have been since 2024. My research focuses on **self-supervised, semi-supervised and multi-modal representation learning for medical imaging**: learning reliable visual representations from limited labels to support diagnosis and disease monitoring. Recent work includes uncertainty-aware semi-supervised medical image segmentation ([CVPR 2025](publications/#dycon-2025), [IEEE JBHI 2026](publications/#ducore-2026)) and a representation-centric way to evaluate 3D medical vision foundation models ([NeurIPS 2026](publications/#reform3d-2026)).

I completed both my **Master's and Ph.D. at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC)** in Chengdu. During my Ph.D., completed in 2023 under the supervision of [Wei Jiang](https://scholar.google.nl/citations?user=oT8Q87YAAAAJ&hl=en), I worked on **visual representation learning for video analysis**. That meant self-supervised and semi-supervised action recognition that stays robust to scene bias and draws on audio-visual correspondence ([IEEE TCSVT](publications/#actorsl-tcsvt-2023), [IEEE TMM](publications/#avcl-tmm-2024)).

Alongside my core research, I collaborate on **applied computer-vision projects** at Khalifa University. These include a **vision-based class attendance system** built on face recognition, and **threat detection in baggage X-ray imagery** with self-supervised and multi-modal learning ([X-ThreatDet, MMM 2026](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-95-6963-2_2)). I have also contributed to international benchmark challenges on **privacy-enhancing sclera segmentation** (SSBC 2025, IJCB) and **3D relief-pattern analysis** (SHREC'25).

I am always happy to discuss collaborations on deep learning for medical imaging, representation learning and applied vision systems. Feel free to reach out.
