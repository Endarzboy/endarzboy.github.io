I am a **Postdoctoral Fellow at [Khalifa University](https://www.ku.ac.ae/)** in Abu Dhabi, UAE. I received my Master's and Ph.D. degrees from the **University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC)**, where I completed my Ph.D. in 2023 under the supervision of [Wei Jiang](https://scholar.google.nl/citations?user=oT8Q87YAAAAJ&hl=en), working on visual representation learning for video analysis.

My research interests lie in **self-supervised, semi-supervised and multi-modal representation learning**, with a current focus on **medical image analysis**: label-efficient segmentation and 3D medical vision foundation models.
